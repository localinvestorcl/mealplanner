Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Family Meal Planner ("we", "our", or "the app") is a meal planning application that helps households plan weekly dinners, generate grocery lists, and save favourite meals. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address and password, used to create and secure your account via Supabase Auth.
  • Household profile: Family size, dietary restrictions, allergies, food dislikes, preferred cuisines, and cooking preferences that you enter voluntarily.
  • Meal plans and recipes: Weekly meal plans, recipes, and grocery lists you generate within the app.
  • Favourite meals and feedback: Meals you save as favourites and any feedback you submit on meals you have cooked.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalised meal plans and grocery lists tailored to your household's preferences.
  • To remember your favourites and improve future plan suggestions.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services to function:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Your data is stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database with row-level security enabled. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — AI-generated meal plans, recipes, and grocery lists. Meal generation requests are sent to Anthropic's API. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Pexels — food photography. Meal names are used to search for relevant food photos. Pexels Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Supabase's hosted PostgreSQL database. All data is protected by row-level security policies, meaning each user can only access their own household's data. Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for use by adults managing a household. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us
