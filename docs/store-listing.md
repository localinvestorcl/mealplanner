## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Take the stress out of "what's for dinner?" — Family Meal Planner uses AI to generate a personalised weekly dinner plan for your household in seconds.

Tell the app about your family — how many people, any dietary restrictions, allergies, food dislikes, and which nights call for something specific — and it builds a 7-day plan that actually fits your life.

**What you can do:**

• Generate a personalised 7-day dinner plan with one tap
• Get a full recipe for any meal, loaded on demand
• Generate a complete grocery list organised by supermarket aisle
• Swap out any meal you don't feel like and get an instant replacement
• Save favourite meals so they rotate into future plans
• Leave feedback after cooking so the app learns what your family loves
• Check off grocery items as you shop — your progress is saved

**Built for real families:**

The app respects your household's rules — meatless Mondays, no red meat on Fridays, kids who won't touch spice, a slow cooker you rely on. Set it once and every plan will follow your preferences automatically.

**Powered by AI:**

Meal plans, recipes, and grocery lists are all generated by Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant. Every plan is unique and tailored to your household — not a generic template.
